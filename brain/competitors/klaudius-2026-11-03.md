## What it is (observed)
- Desktop app (Mac/Windows/Linux) that wraps Claude Code or Codex (OpenAI). Runs on the buyer's own AI subscription.
- Pipeline: Find (Google Maps style search, rated 4+, 15+ reviews, no website / outdated site / booking-platform-only) -> Gather (public photos and data) -> Build (custom site per business, Next.js) -> Deploy (Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, or own server) -> Outreach (email from the buyer's own mailbox) -> Follow-ups -> CRM / inbox.
- Settings: autonomy is "asks first", "auto" (recommended), or "never ask".
- Pitch shape: short plain-text email, one link to the finished site, price named in the first message, line inviting them to say if they do not want more.
- Follow-up: 5-message cadence, daily follow-up round, buyer approves each one in the demo.
- Add-ons to sell: client CMS editor, booking system, live Google rating, go-live SEO/GEO, AI receptionist (demo phone number in the pitch), invoices/quotes, Stripe monthly fees.
- Visit alerts: tells the buyer when a business opens the site (no tracking pixel).

## Pricing (observed)

## Usage / cost per site (observed)
- Only ceiling is the AI plan's usage window. They say top plans can build and pitch about 200 sites a week.
- Entry plans (Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus): quality-check pass is off by default, medium effort.
- Hitting the limit pauses the run until the window resets. Nothing extra is billed.

## Claimed results (vendor claims, unverified)
- Founder: 1,200+ sites built, 180+ replies, 40+ deals in 6 months (UK).
- Geaux Launch: 100+ businesses, 21 replies, 5 clients, $400/site + $100/month.
- Other reviews: $700 + $97/mo; EUR 600 + EUR 25/mo; $2,100 up front + $500/mo from 3 clients; $1,000 AUD for 2 sites.
- Reply mix from 100+ replies (their sample, UK): 52% rejection, 14% warm yes, 13% polite-curious, 10% price question, 8% conditional yes, 3% "who are you?".
- Only about 3% of replies asked for a call. Their deals closed in writing.

## Principles worth testing (inferred, not proven)
1. Lead with the finished site, not "do you want a website?". Their line: "here's the website I built you, want to keep it?"
2. Qualify leads before building (rating 4+, 15+ reviews) so builds are not wasted.
3. Reply within 1-2 hours. Make small edits in about 20 minutes.
4. "Who are you?" reply: name, real city, one sentence on why you built it, easy opt-out.
5. Handle the domain for the client (about $5-6 first year, $10-13 renewal), fold it into the price.
6. Follow up weekly. Most replies come from touch 2 or 3.
7. Take price pushback seriously. They report negotiators were the most engaged and referred others.
8. Sell a monthly retainer (hosting, edits). That is the recurring part.

## When it fails / do not copy
- Testimonials and totals are self-reported. Do not reuse any of them as our proof.
- Auto-send with no human check. Their own demo defaults to asking first.
- No unsubscribe link and no postal address in the email. In the US, commercial email needs both under CAN-SPAM. Check before sending any cold email.
- Vercel free plan is meant for non-commercial use. Check terms before hosting paid client sites there. Cloudflare Pages is the safer default.
- AI receptionist that answers "as" a business the owner has not agreed to. Skip until the owner opts in.
- Their market is mostly UK. US reply rates may differ.
