## Order of work

Brand foundation, then Style Lab rounds, then the component library, then the editing skill that renders against what was locked.

## Why earlier attempts failed (diagnosis)

- Four styles built at once with no locked spec and no target to match. The model averaged them and the result was generic.
- Image-generated stills cannot copy a premium reference frame. Premium comes from real depth, grade and easing, so components get built for real in HyperFrames.
- The people with the premium look execute five basics against a reference: cut dead air, readable first 2 seconds with sound off, one big key word, a change every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds, and a real grade.
