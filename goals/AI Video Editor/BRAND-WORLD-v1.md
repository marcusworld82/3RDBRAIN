Status: Draft for review. Nothing here is approved.

Spelling: LYFE (confirmed by Marcus).

## Scope

LYFE WITH MARCUS is the umbrella brand. COAD, Treasure and the other businesses sit under it.
This file covers the personal creator brand: the face making AI videos for business owners and creators.

## The world: The Construct

In the Matrix, the Construct is the blank room where the Operator loads whatever you need. Marcus is the Operator: the one who has the tools loaded and hands them to you.

Tone: the movies called it. Fiction became real, about 20 years late. Marcus saw it early and shows people how to use the tools right.
Use the idea, not the costume. No code rain, no film logo, no film fonts.

Working name. Marcus can rename it.

## Brand statement

LYFE WITH MARCUS is a late-night workshop run by an Operator. Most people feel run by the system around them. Marcus sits at the console, loads the right tools, and hands them over so you can take control of your own life. Nothing is staged. A little flair shows someone is home.

## The feeling

Target: "I've got this, and I'm not doing it alone."
Marcus's own answer was "I appreciate him." That is what people feel about him after he delivers control. It is the result, not the feeling in their chest.
Open: Marcus to confirm or push back.

## Audience

People who want more control over their life. They know they need a creator to help them across the finish line because nobody is a one-man army.

## What Marcus believes (the lens)

- AI is a tool. Used wrong it harms. Used right it helps.
- Creators who use AI should be applauded because they get to be more creative.
- Movies like The Matrix and The Terminator showed what was coming. It arrived about 20 years later.

Light: one real source, a lamp or screen. Hard shadow on one side.

Composition: sparse. One subject, lots of dark space, one gold word.

## Three themes (proposal, needs Marcus's OK)

- Night: the default. Dark room, warm lamp, focused.
- Day: clean window light, white, beige, gray.
- Signal: neon blue and cyan, glitch, UI. Rare. For AI news and tech reveals.

## The room

Marcus films in a clean basement. The only flat wall has wood panels, not a solid color.
Plan: use the dark, not the wall.
1. Turn off the overhead lights.
2. One warm lamp from the side, about 45 degrees, close to Marcus.
3. Sit 6 feet or more from the wall so the light falls off and the panels sink into shadow.
4. The grade crushes the wood toward deep brown, which sits inside the palette.
5. Offer background removal as an option for scenes with text behind the head.
Test first with one frame. Nothing here is approved.
