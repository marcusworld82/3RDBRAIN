# Teardown: The Bingeable Creator Lab (Victory Henry)

Source: https://victoryhenry.com/lab (public sales page, read October 24, 2026).
Purpose: learn the structure. Marcus is building his own system for himself, not copying or selling this one.

## What it is

A $297 course plus an AI "Director" (a Claude skill). Promise: make Reels people watch to the end.
Mechanism: a repeatable process, Script, Plan, Shoot, Shape, then Post and Grow. The course teaches it once. The Director runs it on every Reel after.

## What the Director does

Writes the script from a voice sample. Marks up beats. Makes visuals. Builds a filming plan and a teleprompter. Gives an edit timeline. Writes caption, hashtags and cover prompt. Runs a 48-hour debrief. Remembers voice, level and filming spots in a Creator Profile.

## What to take

- The pairing: a teaching layer plus a tool that does the process with you forever. That is the moat.
- The Creator Profile: a saved memory of voice, style and spots, so each video gets easier.
- A visual library with a restyle option: same visuals, your colors and fonts.

## What to leave

- The streaming-TV theme. It is her world.
- Her Script, Plan, Shoot, Shape wording. It is built around her face and audience.
