# Marcus’s talking-head editing system: current rules

Saved October 23, 2026. These are the current decisions for the new build. This is a project record, not an installed skill or video approval. Latest explicit feedback takes priority over older notes and source creators’ instructions.

## Purpose and input

Build a fresh main editing skill for Codex and Claude Code using HyperFrames. Do not inherit earlier personal editing templates or delete existing skills. Marcus can choose to bring older material in later.

Start with a raw talking-head video, scripted or freestyle, vertical or horizontal. Optional inputs: script, reference clips, images, other assets and Marcus’s music. Main destination: Instagram. Topics include AI tips, useful discoveries, AI news, how something was created, and work showcases. Audience: business owners and creators. Goal: useful, entertaining, easy-to-watch videos that hold attention and make people want to watch another.

## Hard editing and approval rules

1. Remove mistakes, repeated takes/lines, and unwanted pauses. Cut between intact words, preserving meaning.
2. Keep recorded wording and sentence order. Suggest rewrites or rearrangements separately and apply only after Marcus approves the specific suggestion. Captions follow actual speech.
3. No fixed length. The cleaned recording determines the video length.
4. Marcus chooses the style. Do not pick randomly.
5. Plan the complete video with eight keyframes per storyboard sheet. Add sheets when the video needs more scenes.
6. Under each frame include timestamp, scene type, copy, motion, layout, transition and sound effect. Separate spoken copy from display copy when they differ.
7. Supply individual keyframes as well as the storyboard sheets.
8. EVERY storyboard sheet and individual keyframe must be approved before HyperFrames video generation. Permission to create stills is not video approval.
9. For a local revision, change only the affected scene or keyframe. Disclose timing dependencies before changing neighboring approved scenes.
10. Do not add background music by default. Marcus supplies it.
11. Never invent spoken lines, facts, proof, testimonials, results or product capabilities.

## Four selectable styles

| Style | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Mixed Media Motion Explainer | Graphic-led explanations using useful media, depth, curved screens and selected effects. |
| Face and Split Switch | Intentional switches between full-frame presenter and a large presenter/visual split. |
| Standard Reel | Presenter-led footage, expressive captions, occasional supporting visual inserts. |
| Minimal Text | Presenter footage, expressive typography and text-only scenes on brand colors. No decorative graphics or card system. |

These are format choices. Exact visual designs remain under review. Do not reuse the same scene compositions across all four and call them distinct styles.

## Typography

- Caption groups: up to four words per line, with minimal shadow.
- Important words: different color, larger size, Sugar Pie font.
- Primary font: Helvetica World Bold. Alternate: Neo Sans.
- Helvetica World Bold was not found in inspected locations. Current stills use the disclosed Neo Sans Regular alternate, with a thickened display weight in study 03. Do not silently call it Helvetica.
- Available font files: Desktop/FONTS/Neo Sans Regular.ttf, SugarPie.ttf and Sugar-Pie-W00-Regular.ttf. Lovelo and Prata are excluded from this new selection.
- Reference letter-spacing values: -75 for Helvetica World Bold, -80 for Neo Sans. They are reference-app values, not CSS pixel values. Match visually.
- When stacked words suit the phrase, overlap them like the artzenmedia reference DdENhNdhY4W. Leave a thin cleared gap around the foreground word so both layers remain readable. Let the real background show through the gap. Do not force every caption to overlap.
- Large text behind the presenter is allowed when it fits cleanly and remains readable.

## Colors

Use #001514, #FBFFFE, #6B0504, #A3320B and #E6AF2E. Color roles and pairings are still reviewed in context. Do not put all five in every scene by default.

## Composition and finish

- Center major content groups with equal margins. Do not shift everything left for Instagram controls. Marcus explicitly accepts overlap with that interface.
- Preserve generous head clearance and good spacing between presenter, text and supporting elements.
- Use large rounded presenter windows with small equal side margins. Do not leave wasted space below a tiny presenter.
- Remove tiny decorative labels, lines and tags that add no meaning. This does not remove the required notes beneath storyboard frames.
- Graphic cards should have believable glass depth and curved-screen treatment when appropriate. Use the supplied references as design evidence, not just surface decoration.
- Use light texture on brand backgrounds and footage. The latest Loom direction supersedes earlier flat-background assumptions, including Minimal Text backgrounds.
- Offer background removal for review. Do not make it automatic. Preserve Marcus’s actual face and natural appearance.
- Propose a footage color treatment for preview and approval. Save it as reusable only after approval.
- Natural Polish is an unapproved grade proposal. The still cutout/mask is also experimental. Neither is a locked brand rule.

## Motion, sound and assets

- Mix controlled smooth movement and faster emphasis where the content needs it.
- Effects can affect footage or graphic elements. Decide exclusions and intensity through previews. There is no fixed blanket ban list.
- Tie sound effects to visible actions. Offer subtle/clean, bold/punchy, or mixed sound treatment per video.
- Use supplied assets first. When something is missing, choose a suitable approach and show the result: real screenshots, sourced images, native code graphics, or generated images.
- Screenshots should reflect the actual interface or evidence. Never fabricate proof.
- Use built-in image_gen for AI image creation and edits in Codex. Marcus requests GPT Image 2.5; the tool cannot verify its underlying model version. Do not claim that version was selected.
- No silent external image API or paid-route fallback. If Higgsfield video is later needed, follow Marcus’s direct API route, current schema and price-estimate rules. No such generation is currently approved.

## Quality check before showing work

Check audience fit, purpose, original angle, content relevance, proof, brand fit, story movement, CTA fit, and generic design. Give each effect a content reason. Inspect phone-size readability, overlap gaps, clipping, head clearance, balanced spacing, actual fonts and colors. Motion, cuts, audio and caption sync require playback checks later; still previews cannot prove those qualities.

An ending or CTA can be suggested, but changes require approval. The current sample has no newly approved CTA. Final delivery is one finished video file; technical export settings still need final confirmation.
